# Systematic Reviews and SoKs

## REVIEW-001

- TITLE: Mitigation strategies against the phishing attacks: A systematic literature review
- AUTHORS: Bilal Naqvi; Kseniia Perova; Ali Farooq; Imran Makhdoom; Shola Oyedeji; Jari Porras
- YEAR: 2023
- VENUE: Computers & Security, vol. 132, article 103387
- DOI: 10.1016/j.cose.2023.103387
- SCOPE: Phishing mitigation strategies, technologies, attack vectors, guidelines, and open issues.
- NUMBER_OF_SYSTEMS_REVIEWED: 248 articles, from 2018 through March 2023, across major digital libraries.
- KEY_FINDINGS: The abstract reports a taxonomy of mitigation strategies and identifies open gaps and issues; technology-only approaches do not fully address user capabilities and attack evolution.
- LIMITATIONS: Review scope ends in March 2023 and is not a substitute for primary-paper evaluation.
- RESEARCH_GAPS: Human capability, evolving attack vectors, and open technical issues.
- CAPSTONE_RELEVANCE: Supports explicit evaluation of privacy, generalization, false alarms, and user-facing decisions.
- SOURCE: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cose.2023.103387

## REVIEW-002

- TITLE: Staying ahead of phishers: a review of recent advances and emerging methodologies in phishing detection
- AUTHORS: S. Kavya; D. Sumathi
- YEAR: 2024
- VENUE: Artificial Intelligence Review, vol. 58, issue 2
- DOI: 10.1007/s10462-024-11055-z
- SCOPE: Traditional ML, deep learning, graph-based analysis, network embeddings, generative approaches, and adversarial resilience in phishing detection.
- NUMBER_OF_SYSTEMS_REVIEWED: UNKNOWN from the verified metadata available in this pass.
- KEY_FINDINGS: The abstract emphasizes that static methods are fast but vulnerable to obfuscation, dynamic methods cost more, and poor datasets can make high accuracy dataset-specific.
- LIMITATIONS: Full review extraction and exact inclusion protocol are pending.
- RESEARCH_GAPS: Robustness, dataset quality, dynamic analysis cost, and extracting authentic discriminatory features.
- CAPSTONE_RELEVANCE: Directly supports leakage audits, ablation, temporal/unseen-domain evaluation, and performance measurement.
- SOURCE: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-024-11055-z

## REVIEW-003

- TITLE: A Systematic Review of Deep Learning Techniques for Phishing Email Detection
- AUTHORS: Phyo Htet Kyaw; Jairo Gutierrez; Akbar Ghobakhlou
- YEAR: 2024
- VENUE: Electronics, 13(19), 3823
- DOI: 10.3390/electronics13193823
- SCOPE: Deep-learning techniques for phishing email detection.
- NUMBER_OF_SYSTEMS_REVIEWED: UNKNOWN from the verified metadata available in this pass.
- KEY_FINDINGS: Scope is email-focused and therefore only partially transferable to webpage detection.
- LIMITATIONS: Email threat surface differs from browser webpage evidence; direct transfer is not assumed.
- RESEARCH_GAPS: Webpage-specific, browser-runtime, and privacy-preserving evaluation remain separate questions.
- CAPSTONE_RELEVANCE: Prevents conflating email results with webpage claims.
- SOURCE: https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics13193823

## Use rule

These reviews validate the need for careful datasets, dynamic/robustness analysis, and explicit limitations. Original papers remain the citation authority for specific systems.
